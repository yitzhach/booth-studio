The honest constraint

A monthly fee needs a monthly service

Today the app is local-first: no accounts, no server, no payments. Features that run on your device suit a one-time Pro unlock. A subscription is justified by something that keeps running — hosting, sync, compute, a network of people.

Runs on device

Great Pro features. Hard to charge monthly for.

Needs a server

Justifies monthly. Needs the local-first rule lifted — owner's decision.

Idea 1 · recommended

Show Hub — the show assembles itself

Promoter

Lays out the floor and publishes it as a link.

→

Exhibitors

Claim a booth, design it in 3D. It lands on their booth number.

→

Whole show

Promoter walks the real show, auto rule-checks each booth, exports the walkthrough.

Monthly

Promoters run several shows a year and replace spreadsheets with it. Exhibitors join free → funnel to artist Pro.

No-server v0

“Send my booth to the promoter” file ↔ “Import into booth #N”. Tests demand without breaking local-first.

Idea 2 · most original

Attention map — where the eye actually lands

Runs on device

Simulate passersby walking the aisle, score each work by seconds visible × size.

“Your $4k piece is seen for 1.8 s — swap it with #6.”

On the show floor: footfall per booth, so promoters price corners by data.

Monthly

Ideas 4 & 5

Learn what sells · render it for real

4 · Sales-by-position log

Monthly · sync

Tap a work “sold” on your phone at the show. The app knows its wall, height, light and aisle-facing — after a few shows: “eye-level on the aisle wall sells 3× the back corner.”

5 · AI photoreal renders

Monthly · credits

The render pack is built; artwork is protected so AI never repaints it. Label renders as renders — juried shows want real photos.
